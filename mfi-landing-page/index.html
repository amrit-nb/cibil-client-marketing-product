Learn the key terms related to enquiries mentioned in this notification.

ECN (Enquiry Control Number)

A unique 9–10-digit number generated when the lender checks your CIBIL Score.

Enquiry Purpose

The type of credit requested like home loan, personal loan or commercial loan.

Enquiry Date & Time

When the lender requested your credit history and CIBIL Score or MFI score.
