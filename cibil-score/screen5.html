Enhanced CreditVision Score is the next evolution of the CIBIL Score enabling sharper borrower differentiation for smarter lending.

Key enhancements

  • Improved Predictive Accuracy*: Approximately 20 percent better bad-rate detection and a 22 percent increase in bad-capture rate at comparable approval thresholds.
  • Consumer Friendly: Assess true borrower liability, not just inferred exposure as the new score excludes guarantor trades unless the guarantee is invoked
  • Refined Segmentation: Helps differentiate among borrowers falling within similar risk profiles basis their credit mix, vintage and past performance
  • Scoring basis actual credit events in the borrowers' profile instead of inconsistent reporting, along with the integration of approximately 1500 newly developed algorithms
