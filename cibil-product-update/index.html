These are time-based subscription plans for your CIBIL Score & Report. You can refresh your CIBIL Score & Report daily during the subscription period and have an option to choose either a 1 month (₹ 549/-), 6 months (₹ 1099/-) or 12 months (₹ 1499/-) plan.

Depending upon the Plan that you choose, you also get access to other CIBIL features like CIBIL Alerts, Score Simulator, Personalized Loan Offers, Credit Monitoring and Dispute Assistance.

Select the plan that suits you, click Subscribe, and verify your identity with a few personal details. Once your identity is confirmed, complete the payment online. Your subscription is activated instantly and you can start accessing your CIBIL Score & Report right away.

Credit Monitoring keeps a 24x7 watch on your credit profile and alerts you to key changes — such as new enquiries, new accounts opened in your name, changes to your outstanding balances, or updates to your CIBIL Score.

These alerts help you spot unusual or unauthorised activity early, so you can act quickly and safeguard yourself against potential identity theft or fraud.

You can access your subscription products for the entire duration of the plan you purchase — 1 month, 6 months, or 12 months from the date of activation. During this period you can refresh and view your CIBIL Score & Report along with any other features included in your plan.

Once the plan expires, access is paused until you renew or purchase a new subscription.

Pricing is based on the plan duration you choose and the features it includes. Longer plans offer better value per day — for example, a 6 or 12 month plan works out cheaper on a per-day basis than a 1 month plan.

The price shown is a one-time charge for the full subscription period, inclusive of applicable taxes. There are no hidden or recurring charges unless you choose to renew.

We accept all major payment methods, including credit cards, debit cards, net banking, UPI, and popular digital wallets. All payments are processed securely through an encrypted payment gateway.
